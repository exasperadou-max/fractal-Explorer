How to explore

  • Pan: click and drag or one-finger drag on touch.
  • Zoom in: scroll up, pinch out, click or press +.
  • Zoom out: scroll down, pinch in, right-click or press -.
  • Reset: the "Reset view" button or press 0.
  • Fractal menu: pick a built-in type or choose "Custom" to design your own from a power, folding, conjugate and feedback toggles.
  • Palette menu: pick a preset gradient, or "Custom" for your own.
  • Iteration count rises automatically as you zoom in.
